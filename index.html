About Karise Arts Studio

Located 25 kilometers east of Harare opposite the National Rehabilitation Center at 1 Chiremba Road in Ruwa, Zimbabwe, Karise Arts Studio is a premier gallery and working studio specializing in authentic Zimbabwean Shona stone sculpture. Established in 2000 by internationally acclaimed master sculptor Luxon Karise, the studio has spent over two decades curating museum-quality carvings and mentoring emerging Zimbabwean stone artists.

Born in Guruve and trained under legendary sculptors Bernard Matemera and Tapfuma Gutsa, Luxon Karise has exhibited his work across Europe, North America, and Africa—earning recognition from the National Gallery of Zimbabwe and completing a residency at Chapungu Sculpture Park. Every piece at Karise Arts Studio is hand-carved from locally sourced natural Zimbabwean stones—including green serpentine, springstone, opal stone, and white marble—reflecting the heritage, spirituality, and daily life of the Zimbabwean people.

We welcome studio visits in Ruwa, private commissions, and international art collectors with worldwide crating and shipping available.
